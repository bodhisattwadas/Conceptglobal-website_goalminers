Services

How We Help You

Financial Accounting

“Hands-on”, functional, high-level accounting services refer to an active, deeply involved approach where our team embeds themselves into your daily business operations rather than just reviewing your financial data at the end of the year. Goalminers will act as your "Remote Controller". Unlike traditional, automated, or passive accounting services, a hands-on accountant acts as a collaborative partner. We will handle daily transactions, set up financial systems, and provide real-time strategic advice to guide business decisions.

  • Daily Involvement: Goalminers SBS will manage ongoing tasks like invoicing, bill payments, and payroll instead of waiting for a monthly recap.
  • Proactive Advisory: We strive to predict cash flow shortages, suggest tax strategies, and find cost savings before problems arise.
  • Direct Communication: Clients receive personalized, regular updates and reporting ie Budget vs Actual, KPI Dashboards
  • System Optimization: Prioritized to actively build, clean up, and maintain your accounting software, inventory tracking, and point-of-sale systems.

Audit Readiness

Be prepared before auditors arrive ! Goalminers will help to ensure your company is compliant, and that financial transparency and documentation are in place.

  • Sample Testing: Selections will be made across the G/L to validate transactions in accordance with GAAP and are documented thoroughly
  • Recons and Responses: Confirmations across material balance sheet accounts will be performed.
  • Disclosures and Memos: Technical memorandums and footnotes will be prepared for complex rules, regulations and policies.
  • Audit Assistance: We can be with you during the audit process; work directly with audit team and their PBC selections as needed

Forensic Accounting & Controls

Follow the cash !!! Our team will trace, inspect and ask a lot of questions as to "Who, What and Why".

  • Conflicts of interest: Ensure transactions support the practice of what is in the company's best interest.
  • Embezzlement and Fraud: Allow us to follow the funds and recoup any of your hard-earned assets.
  • Accounting policies and systems: Review and monitor controls are in place with staff responsibilities to verify they work.
  • Litigation Support: Goalminers will calculate all losses and support you in any litigation necessary.

Business Dev Roundtable

Collaborative sessions will be emphasized to align leadership, strategy, and execution. Bring your team together to drive structured growth. We will gather input from leaders across your team that contribute to the organization's mission, opportunities for growth, and promote public support.

  • Health & Retirement: Promoting policies that affect employer-sponsored health insurance, health care affordability, and employee retirement security which drive passion across your organization
  • Education & Workforce: Act as an influence in education and job training initiatives to ensure a competitive labor pool and promotion from within .
  • Market Awareness: Focus on key influences that drive market trends and promote what sets your company above the competition
  • Public Relations: Spread of information between an organization and the public. Core offerings include media relations, crisis communication, brand reputation management, and event planning
